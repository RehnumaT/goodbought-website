Our story

A small family, a big warehouse of good finds.

Goodbought is run by a small local family in Larksville, PA. We buy closeouts, overstock, and liquidated inventory — furniture, home decor, tools, outdoor gear, and more — and pass the savings straight to you, usually at 50–75% off original retail.

Stock changes constantly, so what's here today might be gone tomorrow (and something new takes its place). Stop by, follow us on Facebook, or check back here for the latest.
